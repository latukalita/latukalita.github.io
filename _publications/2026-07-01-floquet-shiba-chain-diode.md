Demonstrates a traveling-wave drive-induced superconducting diode effect with tunable non-reciprocal critical currents and coexisting Floquet Majorana modes, requiring no external magnetic field; analyzed via a self-consistent Bogoliubov-de Gennes formalism.

With K. Roy and Prof. Saurabh Basu, Department of Physics, IIT Guwahati. Manuscript currently in preparation.
