Periodic driving is shown to activate first-order helical edge states coexisting with higher-order corner modes in a Benalcazar-Bernevig-Hughes (BBH) lattice, uncovering a drive-controlled unipolar-to-bipolar skin localization transition characterized via a non-Bloch Floquet generalized Brillouin zone (GBZ) framework.

[Read the preprint on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21954). Currently communicated to Physical Review Research and under review.
