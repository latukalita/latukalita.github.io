Periodic driving is shown to activate first-order helical edge states coexisting with higher-order corner modes in a Benalcazar-Bernevig-Hughes (BBH) lattice, uncovering a drive-controlled unipolar-to-bipolar skin localization transition characterized via a non-Bloch Floquet generalized Brillouin zone (GBZ) framework, real-space chiral winding numbers, and spectral-localizer diagnostics.

With K. Roy, B. Tanatar, and S. Basu. [Read the preprint on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21954). Accepted modulo correction in Physical Review Research.
